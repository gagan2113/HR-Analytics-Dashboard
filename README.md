📊 HR Analytics Dashboard
This project showcases an HR Analytics Dashboard built using Tableau. It provides a comprehensive view of employee attrition and satisfaction trends, segmented by various demographics and job attributes.

🛠 Tools Used
Tableau – for data visualization and interactive dashboard creation

Excel/CSV – assumed as the data source

HR Analytics – domain knowledge applied to analyze workforce trends

📈 Dashboard Highlights
✅ Key Metrics
Total Employees: 1,470

Attrition Count: 237

Attrition Rate: 16.12%

Active Employees: 1,233

Average Age: 37

📊 Breakdown by Category
➤ Department-wise Attrition
R&D: 56.12%

Sales: 38.82%

HR: 5.06%

➤ Age Distribution
Most employees fall in the 30–35 age group.

Peak employee count: 213 at age 33.

➤ Gender-based Attrition
Male: 150

Female: 87

➤ Education Field-wise Attrition
Highest attrition in Life Sciences (89) and Medical (63) fields.

➤ Job Satisfaction Ratings
Highest count of employees rate job satisfaction at level 4.

Top roles with high satisfaction: Research Scientist, Sales Executive, Lab Technician.

➤ Attrition Rate by Gender Across Age Groups
Attrition is higher in males aged 30–35.

Female attrition relatively higher in younger and mid-age groups.

💡 Insights
The R&D department needs immediate attention due to high attrition.

Job satisfaction levels significantly affect employee retention, especially in roles like Sales Executive and Research Scientist.

Age and education background have a strong correlation with attrition trends.
